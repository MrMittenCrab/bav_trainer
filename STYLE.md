# STYLE

Single presentation and language standard for BAV research Markdown, generated figures, and future rendered research. Do not copy this specification into README or individual modules.

## Typefaces

- Latin letters and numerals: Aptos Regular.
- Simplified Chinese: DengXian Regular.
- Regular weight only. Do not use bold or semibold.
- Italics only when the meaning requires it (for example a title of a work). Do not italicize for emphasis.
- Do not vendor, copy, or distribute fonts. Resolve the installed faces at generation time. If a required face is unavailable, report it. Do not silently substitute another family.

## Sizes and spacing

- Rendered research: 14 pt headings, 10 pt body.
- Figures: 11 pt titles, 9 pt labels, captions, and source notes.
- 8 pt between related elements (title and subtitle, series and legend, note lines).
- 16 pt between sections.
- Body line spacing 1.25.

## Color and decoration

- White backgrounds.
- Black and grayscale for all default series, rules, and type.
- At most one optional accent color, used only when a caller assigns it a specific analytical role (for example the latest period). Every figure and research page must remain complete and readable with the accent disabled.
- No gradients, shadows, 3-D effects, decorative backgrounds, ornamental chart chrome, or decorative typography.

## Language

Write direct professional English. One analytical idea per paragraph. Use concrete, useful numbers and ordinary finance words.

Do not use unnecessary framing, methodological narration, repeated caveats, redundant labels, or internal validation terminology in research prose.
