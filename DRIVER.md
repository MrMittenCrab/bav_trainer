# DRIVER

The Driver layer turns reliable financial and operating evidence into a selective argument about what materially affects a business, how its performance is changing, and what remains unresolved. Its output must explain the significance of the evidence without claiming more than the evidence establishes. A reader should understand the argument from the main body; a reviewer should be able to audit it through the appendix and workbook.

This document governs the historical Driver core. Requirements expressed as “must” are mandatory; defaults may be departed from only for a stated analytical reason. STYLE.md remains the authority for presentation and language. Methodological principles attributed below to Valentine or Palepu are distinct from observations about the supplied BAV artifacts. The requirements and architecture are proposed BAV design decisions, not claims that the textbooks prescribe this implementation.

## Research foundation

Valentine is the primary methodological reference. His critical-factor framework concentrates research on consequential factors, tests the exposure through which they matter, and revisits them as conditions change. His two-to-four-factor shortlist and illustrative financial thresholds serve a stock-selection and human-attention problem; they are not universal limits on machine investigation or automatic materiality rules. BAV adopts the selectivity of the conclusion while allowing broad investigation. The distinction between critical business factors and investment critical factors below is a BAV adaptation. [V1, ch. 8]

| Source principle | BAV design decision |
| --- | --- |
| Sector economics, capacity, demand, pricing and relationships with suppliers and customers put company results in context. [V1, ch. 7] | Ask how the business earns revenue, incurs costs and requires capital. Use available contextual evidence to frame mechanisms; retain missing context as a research requirement rather than inventing an industry narrative. |
| Critical factors require material exposure, not merely a noteworthy event; factors and monitoring priorities change. [V1, ch. 8] | Investigate many candidates, qualify their business consequences, and publish the few that explain the material questions. Reopen a question when new evidence changes exposure, interpretation or importance. |
| Independent industry perspectives, purposeful questions and triangulation improve insight; management can be biased or constrained. [V1, chs. 9–10] | Retain source origin, incentives, scope and dependencies. Turn unanswered questions into specific evidence requirements. Automate comparison of available evidence; defer outreach and interviews. Do not copy interview choreography or treat behavioral impressions as evidence. |
| Ratios, charts and statistical relationships can mislead; accounting anomalies are warnings requiring investigation. [V1, chs. 11–12; V2, ch. 14] | Check definitions, denominators, calendars, presentation and plausible alternatives. Preserve anomalies as questions. Neither a yellow flag nor a fitted relationship establishes misconduct or causation. |
| Models should be accurate, understandable, maintainable and concentrated on consequential assumptions; complexity does not establish insight. [V2, chs. 16–17] | Reuse existing calculations and controls. Add a small research representation and selection step, not another financial engine or an exhaustive model of every disclosed item. |

[V3, chs. 23, 25–26] | Select the argument before generating prose. Put decisive evidence and qualifications beside the conclusion, and detailed support in the appendix. Do not import sell-side ratings, promotional language, mandatory forecasts or report styling. |

Palepu supplies the financial consistency discipline: relate strategy and accounting to profitability and sustainability; connect income, investment and cash flow; and distinguish business analysis from valuation and acquisition decisions. Revenue growth alone does not establish value creation. Margin improvement can require capital, and earnings growth can coexist with weaker cash conversion. A claim about returns must use compatible profit and capital definitions. Historical findings can constrain future assumptions without becoming forecasts. [P, chs. 1–2, 9–10, 12–13, 15]

BAV adopts these properties rather than every textbook technique or numerical rule. It must not carry historical accounting conventions, illustrative thresholds, regression cutoffs or scenario probabilities into a company analysis without a valid basis. In particular, an R-squared is not a causal test, and a p-value is not the probability that a hypothesis is true. Five annual observations do not support an unrestricted statistical search or a predictive law. [V1, chs. 11–12; BAV design decision]

## What a Driver is

An economic driver is a condition or business mechanism through which demand, pricing, costs, capacity, competitive position, investment or financing affects performance. The Driver layer is broader: it qualifies evidence about candidate drivers, material outcomes, accounting explanations and unresolved questions. A useful finding need not be causal.

| Concept | Meaning and permitted use |
| --- | --- |
| Economic driver | A business mechanism capable of affecting an economic outcome. Naming a plausible mechanism does not establish that it operated or quantify its effect. |
| Operating KPI | A defined observation about activity or performance, such as store count or comparable sales. Its population and reporting convention govern its use. |
| Proxy | A measurement used to indicate another quantity. Retain the scope difference and conditions under which the proxy may mislead. |
| Accounting identity | A relationship true by construction or accounting definition. It validates consistency, not a business explanation. |
| Mathematical decomposition | A rule allocating an observed change among terms. State the allocation convention and retain interaction or residual terms; another convention can allocate differently. |
| Geographic or segment localization | Evidence of where an outcome changed. It does not by itself explain why it changed. |
| Historical association | Co-movement in a specified sample. It may generate a question or support a limited interpretation, but is neither causal identification nor a predictive law. |
| Management attribution | Management's explanation of an outcome. Preserve the speaker, period, exact scope and qualifiers; do not imply independent verification. |
| System inference | A conclusion derived by BAV from stated premises. Expose its transformation or reasoning and all material dependencies. Calculation alone does not make an inferred quantity a reported fact. |
| Supported economic interpretation | A reasoned account of business significance supported by valid measurements and evidence relevant to the proposed mechanism, with credible alternatives addressed. State the extent of support; do not imply uniquely identified causal magnitude. |
| Unresolved material question | A consequential uncertainty that current evidence cannot settle. Publish it when it changes the reader's assessment, even if it has no point estimate. |

These concepts are not one exclusive label list. Revenue per store is a derived proxy whose multiplication by store count is an identity. A management explanation can concern a plausible economic driver. Retain measurement role, claim type and support separately so that evidence for one proposition cannot silently validate another.

A critical business factor materially affects performance, durability, capital needs, cash generation, returns or risk. An investment critical factor additionally requires an investment horizon, evidence about market expectations, a recognition mechanism or catalyst, and an investment consequence. The historical core primarily establishes or investigates critical business factors. It must not require a stock thesis before publishing useful business research.

For each material finding, retain measurement validity, claim type, proposed mechanism, evidence bearing on that mechanism, materiality, temporal character, the principal competing explanation, and evidence that would change the interpretation. “Mechanism unresolved” is a valid result; an empty field is not a substitute for stating the boundary.

## Qualification and materiality

BAV should investigate broadly across available financial statements, operating KPIs, geographic analyses, accounting judgments and commentary. Candidate questions should arise from acceleration, deceleration, divergence, changing contributions, mix shifts, broken historical relationships, episodic charges, weakening prior drivers and newly consequential constraints. Available business context should also generate questions that numerical anomaly detection would miss, such as exposure to an input cost or a dependence on capital-intensive expansion.

An apparent inflection first creates a question. Before interpreting it economically, check definitions, calendar length, comparison windows, base effects, episodic items, population and currency basis. A reported annual growth rate can remain valid for its reported periods while being unsuitable as an equal-week measure of underlying growth. Do not erase the observation simply because its interpretation needs qualification.

Materiality is a written economic judgment. Each major finding must explain its realized magnitude or conditional exposure and why it changes the business assessment. Consider contribution to changes in revenue, profit, cash flow or returns; durability; structural importance; binding constraints; downside significance; and incremental usefulness to the argument. When quantification is unavailable, describe the exposed activity and consequence without assigning an invented probability or value.

Do not promote a precisely measured but inconsequential ratio above an unresolved issue capable of changing the business interpretation. Conversely, do not call a broad risk material without showing the relevant exposure or explaining why its size is unknown. Conditional arithmetic is useful only when its inputs are supported and its conditions explicit. It is not a forecast or a license to manufacture missing assumptions.

Selection must compare candidates, combine overlapping findings and retain disconfirming evidence. The main-body set should be the smallest set that explains the material changes and consequential uncertainties. There is no fixed factor quota or weighted materiality score. A candidate can be retained for monitoring or supporting detail without receiving a paragraph. Record why a material candidate was selected, combined, deferred or excluded; omission for lack of support must not become a conclusion that the issue is immaterial.

Temporal character belongs to the claim. Distinguish recurring exposure, cyclical behavior, structural change, episodic item and indeterminate persistence. One annual reversal does not establish a structural break; a past impairment disappearing does not establish that the underlying investment risk has disappeared. An identity's stability across periods is mathematical consistency, not evidence of an enduring business relationship.

## Evidence and unresolved research

Verification of a reported fact, support for an inferred quantity and support for an economic mechanism are separate tasks. Preserve the original source and locator, reporting period and availability date when known, definition, population, unit, currency basis, transformation, material qualifiers, and dependencies. A report and workbook repeating the same disclosure are one evidence origin. A management presentation restating a filing is not independent corroboration.

For a reported fact, check faithful representation and scope. For an inferred quantity, check inputs, transformation and uncertainty. For a mechanism, ask whether the evidence addresses the causal link rather than merely the outcome, whether timing is plausible, and whether credible alternatives remain. A reconciled margin bridge supports an accounting explanation; it does not independently establish tariff, markdown or volume effects. Management's quantified estimate remains management's estimate even when its size appears consistent with a reported movement.

BAV may publish a supported interpretation when the mechanism fits the business and the evidence, the reasoning is explicit, and the conclusion survives relevant counterevidence at its stated strength. It must reduce the strength of the claim when alternatives remain unresolved. Numerical causal attribution requires evidence identifying the effect and its basis; neither a perfect identity nor a high correlation supplies that evidence.

Retain alternatives that fit the observations, have a plausible mechanism and would materially change the conclusion. State what observation would discriminate between them. Do not produce a list of imaginable risks. Failure of a hypothesis changes the interpretation, not the underlying valid measurement. A rejected productivity hypothesis, for example, does not invalidate the observed store count.

A research limitation means available evidence cannot establish a stronger claim. A system defect means BAV mishandles evidence actually available, through an incorrect transformation, mapping, status, omission or representation. When the cause of a discrepancy is unknown, record it as unresolved and investigate; do not prematurely assign it to either category. A conflicting definition and a contradictory machine calendar flag require reconciliation, not an automatic assumption that the definitions are equivalent.

Every open material question must retain the strongest supported conclusion, the dependent conclusion that remains unavailable, the missing or conflicting evidence, and the observation needed to reopen it. Stop at that boundary. Do not infer unavailable values, fill gaps with zeros, automatically expand the workbook or acquire new sources to force closure. Block only dependent comparisons and conclusions; unrelated supported findings remain eligible.

Preserve exact reconciliations and tolerances appropriate to units and source precision. An incomplete bridge must show its signed unexplained remainder and must not be described as complete. Omitted disclosure is not a zero-valued component. Rounding is performed after calculation; explanatory estimates keep their original approximation. Arithmetic controls and empirical tests remain distinct. If empirical prediction is later tested, it requires compatible observations, a stated model, information available at the historical test date, meaningful benchmarks and held-out or sequential evaluation. Reconstructing outcomes with inputs derived from those same outcomes is not backtesting a driver.

## Argument and publication

The system must settle the material questions, strongest supportable conclusions, decisive evidence, relevant alternatives, important limitations and overlap before drafting prose or choosing figures. Worksheet summaries must not be concatenated into a report. A publication outline is an ordered set of claims and evidence, not a worksheet list or a filled research form. [BAV design decision; V3, chs. 25–26]

The opening should state what changed, why it matters and the most consequential uncertainty. Subsequent paragraphs should develop that argument through concrete evidence and interpretation. A statement of business significance can be useful without asserting an established mechanism: international growth offsetting domestic weakness, for example, changes the location of dependence even if the reasons for regional performance remain unresolved.

| Layer | Content and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Main body | Material questions and findings, economic interpretation, decisive evidence, integrated figures, material counterevidence, qualifications and consequential unresolved issues. No appendix lookup should be necessary to understand what a central claim means or how far it holds. |
| Appendix | Detailed historical tables and full displayed series; accounting bridges and decompositions; residuals, stability and contradiction tests; definition and calendar compatibility; rejected comparisons; methodology and detailed source/basis notes. Include support needed to audit selected claims and important open questions, not an indiscriminate dump. |
| Workbook and evidence layer | Complete calculations, formulas, full precision, source dependencies, retained candidates and machine-readable validation state. Detailed research fields do not dictate publication headings. |

Detailed numerical tables default to the appendix. A main-body table is justified only when an exact multidimensional comparison is essential and a figure would obscure it. Do not present a table, repeat it in prose, then repeat it in a figure. Prose should identify the important comparison and consequence, not recite a full series.

Each main-body figure must answer a named question and support a material claim through divergence, scale, contribution, inflection, a bridge or another relevant comparison. Choose the figure after choosing the claim. Its specification must identify the source values, transformation, units, population, period, claim supported, necessary qualification and appendix reference. A second figure on the same data is justified only by a distinct analytical question.

Place interpretation next to the figure. Explain what the pattern means, why it matters, what it supports and the relevant boundary or alternative. Use direct labels and common scales where helpful; avoid scale choices that exaggerate or conceal a change. Do not draw a continuous series across incompatible definitions or calendars. Isolated observations with different bases must be visibly separated and labeled, not merely qualified in a remote note. Never combine management estimates and reconstructed accounting contributions into one apparently homogeneous bridge. [V1, ch. 11; BAV design decision]

Use maximal practical flatness: one title and only the sections needed to navigate distinct arguments. Default to no subheadings within the main argument. Introduce changes of idea through topic sentences and transitions; retain headings when removing them would make navigation harder. Appendix sections may group related evidence, but internal field names are not report headings. In DOCX, headings must be semantically marked and styled through STYLE.md rather than manual visual approximations.

STYLE.md controls typefaces, regular weight, sizes, spacing, grayscale treatment and language across Markdown, figures and rendered research. Do not duplicate its specification here or create a parallel style policy. Missing required fonts must be reported rather than silently substituted. Use direct professional English, one analytical idea per paragraph, concrete numbers and ordinary finance terms. Keep internal validation vocabulary in supporting material. State each material caveat where it constrains interpretation; do not repeat generic caveats throughout the report. An appendix must never conceal a qualification that would reverse the apparent main-body conclusion.

## Lululemon applications

The examples below use only the supplied BAV artifacts. They demonstrate design choices, not independent verification of the underlying filings. Figures described here are proposed presentations, not new deliverables. Fiscal labels follow the artifacts: FY2024 ended 2 February 2025 and contained 53 weeks; FY2025 ended 1 February 2026. Monetary examples are USD millions unless stated otherwise. Arithmetic derived here uses disclosed workbook inputs; absent formula caches are not treated as verified outputs.

Store expansion versus company-wide revenue intensity. The question is whether continued expansion is accompanied by stronger revenue generation. In FY2025, company-operated stores increased from 767 to 811, or 5.74%, while consolidated revenue increased 4.86%. Company-wide revenue per period-end store therefore fell approximately 0.83%. Store count is an operating KPI; revenue per store is a proxy; their multiplication and change decomposition are identities. The strongest claim is that footprint grew faster than total-company revenue, warranting investigation of expansion economics. The $607.4 million store-count term in the existing bridge is an arithmetic allocation, not measured revenue from new stores. [B-PDF, p. 1; B-XLSX, Store Count Analysis; Revenue per Store Analysis; Revenue Driver Analysis, rows 8–25, 85–91]

The potential mechanism is weaker demand or slower maturation of added capacity. It is not established: opening dates, regional mix, digital revenue and the 53-week comparison can produce the same divergence. Materiality comes from the coexistence of continuing investment in footprint and slower company growth, not from assuming falling store productivity. The main body should pair the divergence with that boundary. A revenue-growth versus store-count-growth figure is useful, with the unequal-week comparison visible; keep comparable-sales observations separate. Put the count/intensity/interaction bridge, denominator choices and residuals in the appendix. Reopen the productivity question with compatible store-only revenue, exposure through the year, cohort maturation, space and channel evidence. Do not substitute period-end count for average operating exposure.

Comparable sales. The question is how much the existing business supports growth. The artifacts record global comparable sales of 25%, 13%, 4% and 2% for FY2022–FY2025, but with changing channel definitions and comparison windows; FY2022 also has a 16% store-only observation. These are reported operating KPIs. Their coincidence with consolidated growth is descriptive, and positive comparable sales alone does not establish materiality of their contribution to total revenue. The strongest claim is that each period's selected comparable-sales observation is positive on its stated basis. A continuous deceleration claim across all four observations is not admitted. [B-PDF, pp. 1, 9, 18–19; B-XLSX, Comparable Sales Analysis; Revenue Driver Analysis, rows 27–46]

Growth in a continuing population is economically relevant, but price, units, channel mix and changes in the measured population remain alternatives to stronger underlying demand. The exact contribution to consolidated growth cannot be derived without matching revenue weights and exclusions. The main body can use the latest 2% observation to qualify the expansion discussion while stating its coverage; no separate figure is necessary. If several observations are essential, display them as distinctly labeled observations rather than a connected trend. The appendix retains identities, reported versus constant-currency bases, comparison windows and rejected links. Future research requires documented equivalent populations and calendars, revenue weights, and evidence separating price and volume. Revenue growth minus comparable sales must never be labeled new-store contribution.

Geographic revenue and operating profit. The question is whether international growth offsets weaker Americas performance at both the revenue and profit levels. In FY2025, Americas revenue fell $81.1 million while China Mainland and Rest of World increased $393.5 million and $202.1 million. Their contributions to consolidated revenue growth were −0.766, +3.716 and +1.909 percentage points. International growth more than offset the Americas revenue decline. Yet Americas operating profit fell $454.9 million; China Mainland and Rest of World added $191.3 million and $31.0 million, and the corporate/unallocated contribution deteriorated by $62.4 million. These changes reconcile to the $295.1 million consolidated operating-profit decline. This is reported segment evidence and arithmetic localization, not a causal attribution. [B-XLSX, Geographic Segment Analysis, E10:F15, E36:F41, E55:F58 and rows 85–91]

The supported economic interpretation is that the international revenue offset was insufficient to offset the Americas profit decline and higher corporate/unallocated burden. This is material because it explains where the consolidated profit deterioration was concentrated despite overall growth. Lower Americas demand and cost pressure are plausible mechanisms, but currency, product/channel mix, calendar effects and cost allocation can also affect the observed pattern; the mechanism and its persistence are not established. Use aligned, separately scaled revenue-change and operating-profit-change panels, retaining the corporate reconciliation in the profit panel. Explain the different outcomes rather than declaring “China” an economic mechanism. Put complete geographic series, margins, allocation conventions and residuals in the appendix. Future research needs compatible regional price/volume, currency and cost evidence; no segment result should be converted into an organic-growth, standalone-return or synergy claim.

Margin decomposition. The question is which accounting movements explain the reversal in operating margin. FY2025 operating margin fell from 23.7% to 19.9%, a decline of 3.75 percentage points using unrounded ratios. Gross margin contributed −2.62 points, SG&A/revenue −1.09 points, and other operating items approximately −0.04 points; the impairment contribution was zero on the disclosed line. This is an identity and signed decomposition. The strongest claim is that gross-margin contraction and a higher SG&A ratio account for nearly all the reported decline. [B-PDF, pp. 5–7, 17]

The movement is material to the $295.1 million decline in operating profit. Lower pricing realization, higher merchandise or distribution costs, and reduced cost absorption are potential mechanisms, not results of the identity. Cost pressure and mix changes are credible alternatives; the decomposition cannot identify their separate effects. The earlier margin recovery also includes the disappearance of episodic charges, so it cannot be treated as a pure operating-efficiency trend. Use a latest-year operating-margin bridge near the interpretation. Keep full historical ratios, amount bridges, interactions, episodic charges and residuals in the appendix. Future evidence must link specific economic quantities to the relevant cost or revenue base; no residual is to be renamed “mix” or “execution.”

Management-attributed margin pressure. The question is whether the available explanation resolves the margin mechanism. The report records management attributing FY2025 pressure to tariffs, markdowns, occupancy and distribution-center costs, including approximately $275 million of gross-profit reduction from tariffs and de minimis removal. This is an attribution preserved in BAV, with a cited underlying filing locator; the amount is not an independently verified causal estimate. [B-PDF, p. 18, citing LULU_FY2025_Annual_Report.pdf, Form 10-K pp. 28–29, 32–33]

A trade-cost mechanism is plausible and the stated amount is material relative to the profit movement. But it describes an estimated reduction against management's counterfactual, not necessarily the net year-on-year gross-profit change, which was only +$13.3 million. Demand-related markdowns, product mix, offsetting measures and overlapping cost effects could alter its interpretation. The main body should say what management attributes and what remains independently unresolved, next to the accounting margin finding. No additional figure is needed; the estimate must not be inserted into the accounting bridge or used to calculate a supposedly exact share of the decline. Retain the attribution, approximate wording, basis and rounding reconciliation in the appendix. Future research needs the counterfactual, timing, overlap, offsets and corroborating operational evidence before assigning causal magnitude or persistence.

Cash conversion and working capital. The question is whether reported earnings continue to translate into cash and whether working-capital behavior explains the change. Reported CFO fell from $2,272.7 million to $1,602.5 million, while net income fell from $1,814.6 million to $1,579.2 million. CFO/net income fell from approximately 1.25 to 1.01. Inventory increased from $1,442.1 million to $1,700.8 million, approximately 17.9%, against revenue growth of 4.86%. These are reported amounts and derived diagnostics, not an earnings-quality verdict. [B-XLSX, Cash Flow Statement, E26:F27; Income Statement, E20:F21; Balance Sheet, E9:F9; Earnings Quality, rows 5–8]

The cash decline of $670.2 million is material and larger than the earnings decline. Inventory accumulation can consume cash, but growth preparation, sourcing timing, currency, tax movements and other settlement timing compete with a weak-demand explanation. The cash-flow inventory line differs from the balance-sheet inventory change, so those measures cannot be substituted. The available CFO reconciliation rows also do not explain the whole movement: the sum of adjacent changes in rows 17–25 and 27–35 is −$602.9 million, leaving a −$67.4 million difference from the reported CFO change. Preserve that difference without assigning its cause. [B-XLSX, Cash Flow Statement, E17:F35]

The main body should identify weaker cash conversion and the inventory question without attributing the full decline to working capital, seasonality or manipulation. A paired CFO/net-income comparison provides the distinct cash perspective; the inventory observation can remain in prose. The appendix should retain the CFO components and unexplained remainder, inventory movements, accrual and SBC diagnostics, and validated NOWC classifications and decompositions. A balance-sheet NOWC change is not automatically a cash-flow-statement reconciliation. Future research requires the missing reconciliation basis, inventory composition and turnover evidence, tax timing and, if seasonality is asserted, intra-year observations. An incomplete explanation does not block the reported CFO decline.

These six examples are research records, not six mandatory report sections. The expansion and comparable-sales evidence can form one argument; the accounting margin bridge and management explanation can form another. Geography and cash conversion earn space because they add distinct information about where growth and profit changed and how earnings translated into cash. The selected opening should expose those material findings before detailed chronology or methodology.

## Minimum internal design

Use a compact question-centered collection, referencing existing evidence and calculation identifiers. It can live within existing data structures; no database or service boundary is required. Each question needs only the information necessary to qualify and select its claims.

| Part of a question record | Minimum content |
| --- | --- |
| Question and scope | Stable identifier; business question; entity, population and periods; outcome affected. |
| Materiality | Magnitude or conditional exposure where supported; economic rationale; temporal character. |
| Candidate explanations | Proposed mechanisms, relevant alternative and discriminating evidence. |
| Claims and evidence | Claim wording and type; measurement role and validity; source/calculation references; transformations, qualifiers and dependencies; mechanism support and counterevidence. |
| Resolution | Strongest supported conclusion; status of each dependent claim; research limitation or confirmed system defect; specific unresolved requirement. |
| Monitoring | Evidence or change that would reopen the question; affected claims; last assessment and prior interpretation when available. Monitoring is a research requirement, not automatic external acquisition. |
| Publication decision | Main body, appendix, retained only, or excluded; reason; overlap with other questions; selected evidence and figure purpose. |

Keep claim support separate from question closure. A question can remain open while containing valid published measurements. Descriptive findings can be fully supported without an established mechanism. Existing evidence retains its source identity; the question record references it rather than creating another supposedly independent observation.

The processing order is investigation, qualification, selection, then publication. Candidate generation examines available changes and business context. Qualification checks meaning, evidence, materiality and alternatives. Selection resolves overlap and assigns main-body or supporting roles. Publication expresses the selected argument and renders its evidence. These are responsibilities within the existing system, not a requirement for separate services.

Retain the relationship between a selected claim and every material evidence dependency. When an input, definition or interpretation changes, reopen the affected claims and regenerate their dependent prose and figures. Unaffected findings should remain available. A simple list of dependencies is sufficient; no graph infrastructure or numerical belief engine is justified.

## First implementation and deliberate deferral

The first implementation should reuse the current financial calculations, KPI schedules, source references and controls. Add the compact question representation; generate candidates from existing changes and recorded commentary; qualify claim type, materiality, alternatives and bounded limitations; select the argument; and allocate supporting detail before writing. Use the Lululemon examples as regression fixtures for both permitted and prohibited interpretations.

Its first useful publication should surface material findings early, integrate a small number of distinct figures, move detailed tables and testing records into an auditable appendix, and preserve STYLE.md. It should make available geographic-profit and cash-conversion evidence eligible for selection without requiring every unused worksheet to be published. Results must come from the existing verified calculation path; missing formula caches or status labels must not be treated as values or proof of successful validation.

A source or calculation defect required for a selected claim should be repaired in its owning layer and the affected result requalified. Driver must not work around it by inventing an input or maintaining a parallel calculation engine. A research limitation should instead remain open with a specific evidence requirement. Neither category authorizes a broad workbook rebuild.

The historical core should pass downstream qualified claims, evidence, measurement bases, materiality, temporal limits, alternatives and unresolved requirements. It does not pass unsupported default assumptions. Forecasting must separately establish forward assumptions and consistency across revenue, margins, investment and cash flow. Valuation adds a horizon, discounting and terminal assumptions. Stock recommendations add expectations, recognition and risk relative to price. M&A analysis adds acquirer-specific resources, feasible integration effects, costs, timing, financing and consideration. Standalone weakness does not establish realizable synergy or an attractive acquisition price. [P, chs. 10, 12–13, 15; V2, chs. 18, 20]

Defer broad external-source acquisition, automated interviews or outreach, unrestricted statistical search, causal discovery, graph infrastructure, probabilistic confidence scores, exhaustive KPI expansion, forecast- or valuation-engine redesign, consensus ingestion, portfolio construction, stock recommendations, M&A synergy modeling and dashboards. None is necessary to turn the current evidence into a more useful historical argument.

## Acceptance

Acceptance combines deterministic evidence checks with a recorded editorial review. Judgment-based requirements are tested against explicit questions and failure conditions rather than an invented quality score. The following are proposed acceptance tests, not statements that the current system passes them.

| Test | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Early findings | Within the first 250 words, a reader can identify the central supported changes, their business significance and the most consequential unresolved issue. In the Lululemon fixture, the opening captures international revenue offsets, profit deterioration and weaker cash conversion without asserting an established causal model. |
| Claim boundaries | Given the store-count identity and its exact reconstruction, generated prose does not call the count term new-store revenue or the intensity ratio store productivity. A zero residual cannot upgrade the claim to causal. |
| Attribution | The $275 million fixture retains “approximately,” management attribution and the stated gross-profit scope; it is not inserted into the accounting bridge or represented as an independently verified share of the profit decline. |
| Missing and zero | Removing a required observation leaves dependent values unavailable. An explicitly disclosed zero remains zero. Neither expected values, absent caches nor missing lines are silently substituted. |
| Local compatibility | Incompatible comparable-sales or SPSF observations block the dependent trend and chart connection while leaving valid period-specific disclosures and unrelated financial findings available. |
| Calendar and scope | FY2024's 53-week basis is visible wherever material to interpretation. Reported and constant-currency measures, total-company and store-only populations, period-end and average exposure are never silently merged. |
