# Fast Retailing Real-Company Historical Benchmark

## Role

Fast Retailing Co., Ltd. is the canonical **real-company historical benchmark** for Step 9. It is used to test whether the Trainer works on audited disclosures from an actual HK-listed non-financial company rather than only on the illustrative DEMO and synthetic robustness fixtures.

The synthetic DEMO and services/retail/manufacturer fixtures remain necessary for unit tests and deliberately constructed edge cases. They are not replaced by this benchmark.

## Company and period

- Company: FAST RETAILING CO., LTD.
- HK stock code: `6288.HK`
- Primary operating/reporting jurisdiction: Japan
- Accounting basis: IFRS Accounting Standards
- Fiscal year end: 31 August
- Benchmark history: FY2021 through FY2025
- Presentation currency: JPY
- Statement units: JPY millions unless a source table explicitly states otherwise

## Source precedence

Each annual CFS generally presents the current year plus the immediately preceding comparative year. For a fiscal period appearing in more than one supplied audited filing:

1. compare the overlapping reported values;
2. if they agree, use the **latest supplied audited filing that presents that period** as the canonical presentation;
3. if they differ, record the conflict explicitly as a restatement/reclassification/presentation difference before choosing a value;
4. never silently overwrite one audited value with another.

The benchmark fixture is an extraction of the filings, not a reconstruction designed to make the current engine pass.

## Provenance contract

Every numeric value entering the benchmark standardized dataset must be traceable to:

- source PDF filename;
- PDF page number;
- statement or note name;
- source row label;
- fiscal period;
- whether the value is `reported` or `derived`.

A derived value is allowed only when the filings explicitly provide the components and the arithmetic is deterministic. The derivation must be recorded. Missing facts remain missing; do not create zeroes or estimates to satisfy an optional Trainer module.

Preserve source labels where practical. Use `LineItem.concept` to carry stable semantic identity where source presentation creates duplicate labels or year-to-year label changes. Do not rename a source row solely to match an existing resolver alias.

## Expected pressure points

The benchmark is intentionally allowed to expose gaps. The 2025 filing already shows several areas that synthetic fixtures do not adequately test:

- profit attributable to owners versus total profit when NCI is non-zero;
- parent equity versus total equity when NCI is non-zero;
- current and non-current lease liabilities reported separately;
- an explicit aggregate lease liability in the lease note;
- lease interest disclosed separately from total finance costs;
- right-of-use assets and lease cash flows;
- generic financial-asset/liability and derivative rows;
- duplicate display labels that occur in different current/non-current sections;
- explicit PP&E capex and detailed PP&E roll-forwards;
- deferred tax assets/liabilities;
- segment disclosures;
- share-based payments and a historical stock split.

These are evidence for future generalizable fixes. Do not add Fast Retailing-specific branches, label hacks, or hard-coded coordinates to production code.

## Benchmark phases

### Step 9M.0 — baseline

Create a source-grounded five-year standardized fixture, provenance map, source-hash manifest, overlap checks, and a deterministic audit report showing exactly where the current Step 9L.1 engine succeeds or fails. Step 9M.0 is a **measurement checkpoint**; it must not distort production accounting logic merely to obtain a green build.

### Step 9M.1 — acceptance

Use only the documented Step 9M.0 failures to make generalizable accounting/model-contract fixes. Step 9M.1 is complete only when the real-company benchmark can generate a matched Trainer/Answer Key and the applicable historical Check surface passes without Fast Retailing-specific production logic.

After Step 9M.1 is green, every later Step 9 historical feature must preserve the Fast Retailing benchmark when that feature is applicable to the supplied Fast Retailing facts.
